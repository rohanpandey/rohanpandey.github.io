Rohan Pandey

PhD Student · ML & NLP for Science & Healthcare · UMass Amherst

I'm a fourth-year PhD student in the Bio-NLP Lab at UMass Amherst, advised by Hong Yu. In a previous life, I helped develop pre-ChatGPT-era AI platforms for spend analytics at McKinsey (Source AI and Spendscape).

I work on understanding and building AI that holds up when it meets the real world—especially in science and healthcare.

I spend most of my time thinking about:

  • AI for science: how AI can be genuinely useful to scientists, for example helping them find the lesser-known prior work that sparks new ideas (MUSES), and what widespread AI use means for how science is done, validated, and communicated.
  • AI for healthcare: using AI to tackle important public-health challenges, like homelessness and opioid overdose, and understanding what it takes to make AI work in real-world healthcare settings, from messy electronic health records (EHRs) to clinical workflows.
  • Science of AI: understanding what makes AI systems, especially large language models (LLMs), work (or fail), and how we can make them better.
